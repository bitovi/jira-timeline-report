Sidebar footer links: shrink-to-nothing via flexbox (no height media queries)

Drag the dashed box's bottom-right corner smaller/larger. The nav buttons (Sources → Theme) never move or overlap. The footer links give up space to them automatically, disappearing least-important-first — no per-item shrink weighting, no JS, no @media (max-height) breakpoints. Links just overflow the (shrinking) wrapper box top-down and get clipped.

How it works
  1. Nav buttons get flex-shrink: 0 — they're essential and always keep their full height.
  2. The footer links wrapper gets min-height: 0; overflow: hidden so it's actually allowed to shrink below its content size (the default min-height: auto is what causes the original overlap bug). It's the only item in the outer column absorbing negative space, so it's the one that shrinks.
  3. The individual links do NOT shrink (flex-shrink: 0) — their own text already gives them a natural minimum height, so there's nothing to gain by asking them to shrink anyway.
  4. Because they're stacked top-down (default justify-content: flex-start) inside a box that's now smaller than their combined height, the ones at the bottom simply extend past the box's bottom edge and get clipped by overflow: hidden — automatically least-important-first, with zero tuning.
  5. A small fixed gradient at the wrapper's bottom edge softens whichever item happens to straddle the cutoff, so it fades instead of hard-clipping mid-glyph.
