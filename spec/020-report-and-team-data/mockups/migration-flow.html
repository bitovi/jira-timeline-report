What a record looks like in Jira afterward

The purple block is the entity property — the source of truth, invisible in Jira's UI, 32 KB of its own. The description above it is a rendered view that the app never reads; it exists so Jira's issue history has something meaningful to diff, and so a human opening the issue understands what it is. The team issue is where TR-133 gets interesting. The rendered table isn't decoration here — the Jira Auto Scheduler reads a table like it out of the config issue today (src/jira/storage/index.web.ts:128-155). Moving team data to TEAMMAP either keeps writing that table in its old location, or needs a coordinated change in the Auto Scheduler. That's why Phase 6 is blocked rather than merely last.
